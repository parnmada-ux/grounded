Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 31, 2026

Plain-English summary (read this first):

MindoDay collects the minimum information needed to give you a private space to reflect: your email, a hashed password, the daily reflections you choose to write, and your subscription status. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't share your reflections with anyone. Your reflections are visible only to you.

If you delete your account, your data is permanently erased after a 30-day cooling-off period. If you have any questions, write to hello@mindoday.com. For privacy-specific requests, write to privacy@mindoday.com.

1. Who we are

MindoDay is operated by Parnmada Nitinakprach, based in Bangkok, Thailand. In this policy, "we," "us," and "MindoDay" refer to the operator. "You" means the person using MindoDay.

MindoDay founded: May 22, 2026.

For any privacy-related question, request, or concern, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@mindoday.com (PDPA Section 41 designated contact). For general inquiries, write to hello@mindoday.com.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what's necessary to provide the service.

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Email address — used to identify your account and to send essential communications (account verification, subscription receipts, recovery emails).
  • Password — never stored in plain text. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores only a one-way cryptographic hash.
  • Daily reflections, journal entries, and any text you write in MindoDay — stored privately and visible only to you.
  • Profile preferences — including any settings you change inside the app.

2.2 Information collected automatically when you use MindoDay

  • Subscription state — your current plan (Free / Premium / Pro), trial dates, renewal dates.
  • Basic technical information — browser type, operating system, language preference, and approximate region — used to keep the service running and to debug errors.
  • Authentication events — sign-in time, sign-out time, account creation date.

2.3 Information collected by Stripe (our payment processor)

If you subscribe to a paid plan, Stripe processes your payment. Stripe receives your card details directly — we never see or store your full card number, CVV, or full card expiry. We do receive from Stripe:

  • A customer ID and subscription ID that link your MindoDay account to your Stripe subscription
  • The last 4 digits of the card and the brand (e.g., "Visa ending 4242")
  • The country the card was issued in
  • Subscription status (active, past due, cancelled)

Stripe's own privacy policy applies to data they collect: https://stripe.com/privacy

2.4 Information we do not collect

  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not use social media login analytics.
  • We do not collect precise location data (GPS).
  • We do not collect biometric data.
  • We do not read your contacts, microphone, camera, or other phone sensors.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  1. Provide the service — show you your account, your reflections, and your subscription status.
  2. Authenticate you securely — make sure only you can sign in to your account.
  3. Process payments — through Stripe.
  4. Send essential communications — account verification, password resets, subscription receipts, and notices about changes to this policy or our terms.
  5. Improve and debug MindoDay — fix bugs, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.
  6. Comply with legal obligations — for example, retaining payment records as required by Thai tax law.

We do not use your reflections, journal entries, or any personal content to train AI models, sell advertising, or share with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. How long we keep your information

  • While your account is active: we keep your information as long as your account exists.
  • If you delete your account: MindoDay uses a 30-day cooling-off period. Your data is marked for deletion immediately, but actual erasure happens after 30 days. During that window, you can restore your account by signing in. After 30 days, all identifiable data is permanently deleted, including reflections and account credentials.
  • Payment records: kept for 7 years after the last transaction, as required by Thai accounting and tax law (Revenue Code, Section 87).
  • Backups: routine backups may retain deleted data for up to 35 additional days after final deletion before being overwritten in the normal backup rotation.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct — update inaccurate information (most settings are editable from inside MindoDay).
  • Delete — permanently delete your account and reflections (Settings → Account → Delete Account).
  • Restrict or object — ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Portability — request your reflections in a machine-readable format (JSON or PDF).
  • Withdraw consent — for anything we process based on your consent. (Withdrawing consent does not affect lawful processing before withdrawal.)
  • Lodge a complaint — with the Personal Data Protection Committee of Thailand (PDPC) or your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@mindoday.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

MindoDay is not intended for use by children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact privacy@mindoday.com and we will delete the account.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption in transit — all communication between your device and MindoDay uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Encryption at rest — Firebase and Stripe encrypt stored data using AES-256 or equivalent.
  • Authentication — passwords are hashed with bcrypt or PBKDF2 (handled by Firebase Authentication).
  • Access controls — only authorized backend services can read user reflections, and there is no internal staff dashboard that exposes individual reflection content.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever experience a data breach that affects you, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by PDPA. To report a security concern or vulnerability, please write to security@mindoday.com.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

MindoDay uses a minimal set of essential cookies and local storage:

  • Authentication tokens — to keep you signed in.
  • Local app state — to remember your preferences and prevent re-fetching data on every page load.

We do not use third-party analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels.

10. Third-party links

MindoDay may occasionally link to external resources (mental health hotlines, partner content, support articles). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites. Please read their policies before sharing information with them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email at least 14 days before they take effect, and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any change. Continuing to use MindoDay after a change means you accept the updated policy.
